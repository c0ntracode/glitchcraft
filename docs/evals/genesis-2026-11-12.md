# Genesis walkthrough, 2026-11-12 (self-evaluation, no fresh agents)

The author asked for no subagents, so these four scenarios were walked through by the session that wrote the code, following `SKILL.md` literally with the real commands on the author's dump (Sonic the Hedgehog REV01 `[h3C]`, SHA-1 `1f1e480f…`, region field blank, i.e. a hacked build). This is weaker evidence than the NES and SNES runs: the author knows the answers. It checks that the commands and wording work end to end, not that a fresh agent would follow the skill.

## What the work turned up

- `vet`'s `unofficial-opcode-follows` caught a real mistake in my own first infinite-lives patch: replacing the 4-byte `SUBQ` with a 2-byte `NOP` leaves its extension word `FE12` to be executed as a line-F instruction. The flag also gave a false positive for the corrected `BRA.S` patch until `vet` was changed to look only at instructions reached in sequence (it now stops at an unconditional branch, jump or return). Both cases have tests.
- The public Sonic 1 codes (`GJ6A-CA7A` and others) land on unrelated instructions in this dump, as expected for a different build; `vet` shows it (`MOVE.B $26(A0),D0 -> BRA.S`, whose target decodes as `dc.w`).
